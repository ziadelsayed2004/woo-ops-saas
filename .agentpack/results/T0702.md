## Security, tenancy, money/time and idempotency review

- Analytics filters are strict Zod fields; SQL uses fixed JSON paths and bound parameters, including escaped LIKE values. No raw SQL, field paths, or expressions are accepted from the client.
- Fact and cost-coverage queries require the authenticated account ID. No organization/account ID is accepted from the UI.
- Metrics remain integer minor units and currency-separated; the dashboard never adds currencies.
- Fact timestamps remain UTC/source-derived and are displayed through locale-aware formatting.
- The connector remains read-only and dashboard operations do not expose remote mutation methods.
- Cost coverage is explicitly labelled account-wide snapshot coverage; it is not represented as an exact date/facet-level coverage percentage.

## Known limitations and follow-up tasks

- The premium Woo export extension stores its global status in protected metadata that the standard Woo REST response may omit. This build reports that state as unavailable instead of fabricating a value. A separate read-only WordPress endpoint is required if the store confirms that the extension does not expose the field.
- Refund snapshots are attributed to the original order date and are not allocated to individual order lines. The UI explicitly states this difference from Woo Analytics' refund-date reporting.

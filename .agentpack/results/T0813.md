## Security, tenancy, money/time and idempotency review

- Every new read/write path derives `accountId` from the authenticated session; mutation routes require the existing operation/admin role and CSRF/origin protections.
- Cost overrides require an authenticated mutation actor, an account-owned order/line, matching ISO currency, a bounded decimal minor-unit string, and a non-empty reason; audit metadata excludes credentials and raw payloads.
- Durable job payloads are schema-validated and bounded; rebuild keys are deterministic for scope plus caller key, and backfill/maintenance jobs use the existing retry/dead-letter machinery.
- Connector actions remain read-only; the new administration UI never exposes remote order, stock, product, customer, or status mutation.
- Dates remain UTC and money remains integer minor units with currency separation.

## Security, tenancy, money/time and idempotency review

- Session and CSRF tokens are random; only hashes persist.
- Mutating requests reject mismatched origins when an Origin header is present.
- Logout requires a valid session and matching CSRF token.
- Registration account creation is one SQLite transaction.
- Rate limiting is process-local and is scheduled for durable/security hardening in T0802.

## Known limitations and follow-up tasks

- Password reset, MFA, explicit account permission middleware and session revocation on privilege/password change belong to T0103/T0802.
- Cookies are currently not marked `Secure` on local HTTP; production cookie policy will derive from HTTPS deployment configuration.
- Tests are currently smoke-backed; dedicated auth unit/integration/security test runners are follow-up hardening work.
